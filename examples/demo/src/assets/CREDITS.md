## Important: the AI metadata in the demo is fictional

**None of these images is AI-generated or AI-modified.** The `ai={{ … }}` metadata attached to them
on the scenario pages — including `kind: "generated"` and `scope: "deepfake"` — is illustrative,
written purely to exercise the disclosure logic under each policy mode. Every scenario page states
this on the page itself.

Do not copy a scenario page's metadata onto a real asset without checking that it describes that
asset truthfully. Declaring AI involvement that did not happen is as wrong as omitting it.
